Process
04 principles
01

Start in the data,
not the deck.

Every project begins with what's already known: analytics, support tickets, prior research, the team's own intuitions. The design problem isn't visible until I've sat with the evidence.

Discovery
02

Make the small thing
first.

Strategy and shipped pixels are the same conversation. Earning trust to do bigger work means landing the smaller version cleanly first: a single screen, a single flow, a single decision that holds up in production.

Method
03

Detail is
the product.

A great interaction isn't the wireframe; it's the handful of micro-decisions made between Figma and the engineer's pull request. Most of the value lives in the last 10%.

Craft
04

Decide once,
document forever.

Design that scales is design that's traceable. Every recurring decision deserves a written rationale, not because anyone will read it, but because the act of writing it down is what makes it sturdy.

I build
digital products
with intent.

My path into product design started in visual and editorial design, and evolved through freelance work and in-house roles into full-scope product design at MyLoyal in Copenhagen, where I've spent the last 5+ years shaping loyalty and engagement experiences for brands across Denmark. That graphic design foundation still shows up in my work today: a stronger-than-average eye for visual craft, typography, and motion, layered on top of research, systems thinking, and interaction design.

Every project starts with the same question: what's actually happening for the person using this, and why? I work iteratively, sketching, testing, refining, staying close to design trends without chasing them, and I'm most useful in the messy middle of a project, turning ambiguous problems into shipped systems that hold up in production. Calm under pressure, observant, and happiest working closely with a team rather than in isolation.
